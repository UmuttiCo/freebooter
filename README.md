# Freebooter

A pirate crew drama sim, in the spirit of This War of Mine and Darkest Dungeon rather than a tycoon game. You never have enough able hands: men get wounded, sick, drunk, exhausted or bitter, and every choice about who you push tonight is paid for later by a named person rather than a meter.

**[Play it in the browser](https://umuttico.github.io/freebooter/)**

One run: sail from Port Royal on a three leg patrol, take a contract, handle what the sea and the crew throw at you, raid or hold back, come home and do it again. Gold is only a war chest. Infamy is the spine, and it ends one of two ways, a king's pardon or the crown and the noose.

Built solo in Godot 4. Design, systems, writing and art assembly are mine, art is composited from CC0 and CC-BY pixel sets (Kenney, LPC / OpenGameArt).

## About this repository

This holds the exported web build only, which is why it is all `index.*` files. The Godot project source lives in a private repository. The build is a snapshot rather than a continuously deployed one.

It is a large download, around 130 MB of WebAssembly and packed assets, so give it a moment on the first load. Desktop browsers only.
